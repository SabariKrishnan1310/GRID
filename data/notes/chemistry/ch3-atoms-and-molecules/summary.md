## Laws of Chemical Combination

### Law of Conservation of Mass
- Mass is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction
- Total mass of reactants = Total mass of products
- Proved by **Lavoisier** (1774)

### Law of Constant Proportions
- A chemical compound always contains same elements in fixed ratio by mass
- Proved by **Joseph Proust** (1799)
- Example: Water always has H:O = 1:8 by mass

## What is an Atom?

- Smallest particle of an element that takes part in a chemical reaction
- Atoms are very small — radius = 10⁻¹⁰ m (1 angstrom)
- Atoms are indivisible (in chemical reactions)
- Dalton's Atomic Theory: atoms of same element are identical; compounds are formed by combination of atoms of different elements in simple ratio

## What is a Molecule?

- Group of two or more atoms bonded chemically
- Smallest unit of a compound that retains properties
- **Molecule**: Atoms of same element → e.g., O₂, H₂, N₂
- **Molecule**: Atoms of different elements → e.g., H₂O, CO₂, NaCl

## Atomicity
- Number of atoms in a molecule
- **Monoatomic**: He, Ne, Ar
- **Diatomic**: H₂, O₂, N₂, Cl₂, Br₂, I₂
- **Triatomic**: O₃, H₂O
- **Tetratomic**: P₄, S₈ (actually polyatomic)

## Ions
- Atom or group of atoms with positive or negative charge
- **Cation**: Positively charged (loss of electrons) → Na⁺, Ca²⁺, Al³⁺
- **Anion**: Negatively charged (gain of electrons) → Cl⁻, O²⁻, SO₄²⁻

## Writing Chemical Formulae

- Based on **valency** of elements
- Cross-over method: swap valencies as subscripts
- **NaCl**: Na (valency 1) + Cl (valency 1) → Na₁Cl₁ = NaCl
- **CaCl₂**: Ca (valency 2) + Cl (valency 1) → CaCl₂
- **Al₂O₃**: Al (valency 3) + O (valency 2) → Al₂O₃

## Polyatomic Ions
- Group of atoms with charge
- **SO₄²⁻**: Sulphate
- **CO₃²⁻**: Carbonate
- **OH⁻**: Hydroxide
- **NO₃⁻**: Nitrate
- **NH₄⁺**: Ammonium

## Molecular Mass
- Sum of atomic masses of all atoms in a molecule
- Example: H₂O = 2(1) + 16 = 18 u

## Formula Unit Mass
- For ionic compounds: sum of atomic masses of formula unit
- Example: NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 u

## Mole Concept
- 1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles (Avogadro's number)
- 1 mole of any substance = its molecular mass in grams
