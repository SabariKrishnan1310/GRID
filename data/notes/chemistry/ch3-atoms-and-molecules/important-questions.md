## Important Formulas

### Chemical Formulae

$$
\text{Molecular mass} = \sum (\text{Atomic mass of all atoms})
$$

$$
\text{Formula unit mass} = \sum (\text{Atomic masses in formula unit})
$$

$$
\text{Number of moles} = \frac{\text{Given mass}}{\text{Molar mass}}
$$

$$
\text{Number of particles} = \text{Number of moles} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}
$$

## Important Questions

### Q1. State the Law of Conservation of Mass. Give one example.

**Answer:**
- Mass is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction
- Total mass of reactants = Total mass of products
- **Example**: When 34 g AgNO₃ reacts with 17 g NaCl:
  - Products: 143.5 g AgCl + 17 g NaNO₃
  - Total reactants (51 g) = Total products (51 g)

---

### Q2. State the Law of Constant Proportions. Give one example.

**Answer:**
- A chemical compound always contains the same elements in the same proportion by mass
- **Example**: Water always contains H:O = 1:8 by mass (or 11.2% H, 88.8% O)
- This is true regardless of the source of water

---

### Q3. What is the difference between an atom and a molecule?

| Atom | Molecule |
|------|----------|
| Smallest particle of an element | Smallest particle of a compound |
| May or may not exist independently | Always exists independently |
| Example: Fe, O, Na | Example: H₂O, CO₂, NaCl |
| Does not have formula | Has a chemical formula |

---

### Q4. Define atomicity. Give examples of monoatomic, diatomic, triatomic, and polyatomic molecules.

**Answer:**
- **Atomicity**: Number of atoms in one molecule
- **Monoatomic**: He, Ne, Ar (single atoms)
- **Diatomic**: H₂, O₂, N₂, Cl₂, Br₂, I₂
- **Triatomic**: O₃ (ozone)
- **Polyatomic**: P₄, S₈

---

### Q5. What are ions? How are they formed?

**Answer:**
- **Ion**: An atom or group of atoms with positive or negative charge
- Formed by **gain or loss of electrons**
- **Cation**: Positive ion → formed by loss of electrons (Na → Na⁺ + e⁻)
- **Anion**: Negative ion → formed by gain of electrons (Cl + e⁻ → Cl⁻)

---

### Q6. Write the chemical formulae of:
(a) Magnesium chloride
(b) Aluminium oxide
(c) Calcium sulphate
(d) Sodium carbonate

**Answer:**
- MgCl₂: Mg²⁺ (valency 2) + 2Cl⁻ (valency 1)
- Al₂O₃: 2Al³⁺ (valency 3) + 3O²⁻ (valency 2)
- CaSO₄: Ca²⁺ (valency 2) + SO₄²⁻ (valency 2)
- Na₂CO₃: 2Na⁺ (valency 1) + CO₃²⁻ (valency 2)

---

### Q7. Calculate the molecular mass of:
(a) H₂O
(b) CO₂
(c) H₂SO₄
(d) C₆H₁₂O₆

**Answer:**
- H₂O = 2(1) + 16 = **18 u**
- CO₂ = 12 + 2(16) = **44 u**
- H₂SO₄ = 2(1) + 32 + 4(16) = **98 u**
- C₆H₁₂O₆ = 6(12) + 12(1) + 6(16) = **180 u**

---

### Q8. Calculate the formula unit mass of:
(a) NaCl
(b) CaCl₂
(c) Al₂O₃

**Answer:**
- NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = **58.5 u**
- CaCl₂ = 40 + 2(35.5) = **111 u**
- Al₂O₃ = 2(27) + 3(16) = **102 u**

---

### Q9. What are polyatomic ions? Give examples.

**Answer:**
- Group of atoms that carry a positive or negative charge
- **SO₄²⁻**: Sulphate
- **CO₃²⁻**: Carbonate
- **OH⁻**: Hydroxide
- **NO₃⁻**: Nitrate
- **NH₄⁺**: Ammonium

---

### Q10. If 36 g of water contains 4 g of hydrogen, what is the percentage of hydrogen in water?

**Answer:**
- Percentage of hydrogen = (4/36) × 100 = **11.11%**
- Percentage of oxygen = 100 − 11.11 = **88.89%**
- This verifies the Law of Constant Proportions

---

### Q11. 3.01 × 10²³ atoms of an element X have a mass of 6 g. Find the atomic mass of X.

**Answer:**
- 3.01 × 10²³ atoms = **0.5 moles** (since 6.022 × 10²³ = 1 mole)
- Mass of 1 mole = 6 / 0.5 = **12 g/mol**
- Atomic mass of X = **12 u** (element is Carbon)

---

### Q12. Which has more molecules: 18 g of water or 46 g of ethanol (C₂H₅OH)?

**Answer:**
- Water (H₂O): Molecular mass = 18 u → 18 g = 1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ molecules
- Ethanol (C₂H₅OH): Molecular mass = 46 u → 46 g = 1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ molecules
- **Both have the same number of molecules** (1 mole each)
