## Distance vs Displacement

- **Distance**: Total path length covered → scalar (no direction)
- **Displacement**: Shortest straight-line distance from initial to final position → vector (has direction)
- Distance ≥ Displacement (always)
- Both have same SI unit: **metre (m)**

## Speed

- **Speed**: Distance covered per unit time → scalar
- Speed = Distance / Time
- SI unit: **m/s**
- Uniform speed: equal distances in equal intervals of time
- Non-uniform speed: unequal distances in equal intervals of time

## Velocity

- **Velocity**: Displacement per unit time → vector
- Velocity = Displacement / Time
- SI unit: **m/s**
- Uniform velocity: equal displacements in equal intervals of time
- Non-uniform velocity: unequal displacements in equal intervals of time

## Acceleration

- **Acceleration**: Rate of change of velocity → vector
- Acceleration = (Final velocity - Initial velocity) / Time
- SI unit: **m/s²**
- Uniform acceleration: equal change in velocity in equal time intervals
- Non-uniform acceleration: unequal change in velocity in equal time intervals

## Distance-Time Graph

- **Uniform speed**: Straight line passing through origin (slope = speed)
- **Non-uniform speed**: Curved line
- **At rest**: Straight line parallel to time axis (slope = 0)
- Slope of distance-time graph = speed

## Velocity-Time Graph

- **Uniform acceleration**: Straight line with non-zero slope (slope = acceleration)
- **Non-uniform acceleration**: Curved line
- **Zero acceleration (uniform velocity)**: Straight line parallel to time axis
- **Distance covered** = Area under velocity-time graph
- Slope of velocity-time graph = acceleration

## Equations of Motion

For uniformly accelerated motion:

$$
v = u + at
$$

$$
s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2
$$

$$
v^2 = u^2 + 2as
$$

Where: u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, t = time, s = displacement

## Uniform Circular Motion

- Motion in a circular path with constant speed
- Velocity changes continuously (direction changes)
- Acceleration is always directed towards the center (centripetal acceleration)
- Time for one complete revolution = **time period**
- Speed = 2πr / Time period
