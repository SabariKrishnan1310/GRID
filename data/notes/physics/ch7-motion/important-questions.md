## Important Formulas

$$
\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{d}{t}
$$

$$
\text{Velocity} = \frac{\text{Displacement}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{\Delta s}{t}
$$

$$
\text{Acceleration} = \frac{\text{Final velocity} - \text{Initial velocity}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{v - u}{t}
$$

$$
\text{Average speed} = \frac{\text{Total distance}}{\text{Total time}}
$$

$$
\text{Average velocity} = \frac{\text{Total displacement}}{\text{Total time}}
$$

### Equations of Motion (Uniformly Accelerated Motion)

$$
v = u + at
$$

$$
s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2
$$

$$
v^2 = u^2 + 2as
$$

### Circular Motion

$$
\text{Speed} = \frac{2\pi r}{T} = 2\pi rf
$$

$$
\text{Time period} = \frac{2\pi r}{v}
$$

$$
\text{Frequency} = \frac{1}{\text{Time period}} = \frac{1}{T}
$$

## Important Questions

### Q1. A car travels 100 km in 2 hours and returns in 3 hours. Find average speed and average velocity.

**Answer:**
- Total distance = 100 + 100 = 200 km
- Total time = 2 + 3 = 5 hours
- Average speed = 200/5 = **40 km/h**
- Total displacement = 0 (returned to starting point)
- Average velocity = 0/5 = **0 km/h**

---

### Q2. A body starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2 m/s² for 5 s. Find the distance covered and final velocity.

**Answer:**
- u = 0, a = 2 m/s², t = 5 s
- v = u + at = 0 + 2 × 5 = **10 m/s**
- s = ut + ½at² = 0 + ½ × 2 × 25 = **25 m**

---

### Q3. A body moving with uniform acceleration covers 100 m in 10 s. If its initial velocity is 5 m/s, find the acceleration.

**Answer:**
- s = 100 m, t = 10 s, u = 5 m/s
- s = ut + ½at²
- 100 = 5 × 10 + ½ × a × 100
- 100 = 50 + 50a
- 50 = 50a → a = **1 m/s²**

---

### Q4. A car moving at 20 m/s decelerates at 4 m/s². How long does it take to stop?

**Answer:**
- u = 20 m/s, v = 0 (stops), a = -4 m/s²
- v = u + at
- 0 = 20 + (-4)t
- 4t = 20 → t = **5 s**

---

### Q5. A body is thrown vertically upwards with velocity 49 m/s. Find the maximum height reached and time to reach maximum height. (g = 9.8 m/s²)

**Answer:**
- u = 49 m/s, v = 0 (at maximum height), a = -g = -9.8 m/s²
- At maximum height: v = u - gt → 0 = 49 - 9.8t → t = **5 s**
- Maximum height: v² = u² - 2gh → 0 = 49² - 2 × 9.8 × h
- h = 2401/19.6 = **122.5 m**

---

### Q6. A car travels 60 km north in 1 hour, then 40 km east in 1 hour. Find:
(a) Total distance
(b) Total displacement
(c) Average speed
(d) Average velocity

**Answer:**
- (a) Total distance = 60 + 40 = **100 km**
- (b) Displacement = √(60² + 40²) = √(3600 + 1600) = √5200 = **72.1 km** (at angle tan⁻¹(40/60) = 33.7° east of north)
- (c) Average speed = 100/2 = **50 km/h**
- (d) Average velocity = 72.1/2 = **36.05 km/h**

---

### Q7. What is the distance traveled by an object in uniform circular motion in one revolution? What is its displacement?

**Answer:**
- Distance in one revolution = **circumference = 2πr**
- Displacement in one revolution = **0** (returns to starting point)
- Example: For r = 7 m, distance = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = 44 m, displacement = 0

---

### Q8. The distance-time graph of two cars A and B are shown. Which car is faster?

**Answer:**
- Car with **steeper slope** on distance-time graph is faster
- Slope = speed
- If car A has steeper slope → car A is faster
- Need to calculate slopes: speed = distance/time for each car

---

### Q9. A train 100 m long passes a pole in 10 s. Find its speed.

**Answer:**
- Distance = length of train = 100 m
- Time = 10 s
- Speed = Distance/Time = 100/10 = **10 m/s = 36 km/h**

---

### Q10. A body moves in a circle of radius 5 m with speed 22 m/s. Find:
(a) Time period
(b) Frequency
(c) Centripetal acceleration

**Answer:**
- (a) Time period T = 2πr/v = 2 × 22/7 × 5 / 22 = 10/7 = **1.43 s**
- (b) Frequency f = 1/T = 7/10 = **0.7 Hz**
- (c) Centripetal acceleration a = v²/r = 22²/5 = 484/5 = **96.8 m/s²**
