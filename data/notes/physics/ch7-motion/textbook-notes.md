## 7.1 Introduction

- **Motion**: Change in position of an object with respect to time and surroundings
- An object is said to be in motion if its position changes with time
- An object is at rest if its position does not change with time
- Motion is relative - depends on the reference point chosen

## 7.2 Distance and Displacement

- **Distance**: Total path length covered during motion
  - Scalar quantity (has magnitude only, no direction)
  - Always positive
  - SI unit: metre (m)
  - Example: A car travels 5 km east then 3 km west → total distance = 8 km

- **Displacement**: Shortest straight-line distance from initial position to final position
  - Vector quantity (has both magnitude and direction)
  - Can be positive, negative, or zero
  - SI unit: metre (m)
  - Example: Same car → displacement = 5 - 3 = 2 km east

- Distance ≥ Displacement (distance is always greater than or equal to displacement)

## 7.3 Speed and Velocity

- **Speed**: Distance covered per unit time
  - Speed = Distance / Time = d/t
  - Scalar quantity
  - SI unit: m/s
  - Average speed = Total distance / Total time
  - **Uniform speed**: Equal distances covered in equal intervals of time
  - **Non-uniform speed**: Unequal distances covered in equal intervals of time

- **Velocity**: Displacement per unit time
  - Velocity = Displacement / Time = Δs/t
  - Vector quantity (has direction)
  - SI unit: m/s
  - Average velocity = Total displacement / Total time
  - **Uniform velocity**: Equal displacements in equal intervals of time
  - **Non-uniform velocity**: Unequal displacements in equal intervals of time

## 7.4 Acceleration

- **Acceleration**: Rate of change of velocity
  - Acceleration = (Final velocity - Initial velocity) / Time
  - a = (v - u) / t
  - SI unit: m/s²
  - Vector quantity
  - **Uniform acceleration**: Equal change in velocity in equal time intervals
  - **Non-uniform acceleration**: Unequal change in velocity in equal time intervals
  - **Retardation/Deceleration**: Negative acceleration (velocity decreases)

## 7.5 Equations of Motion

For an object moving with **uniform acceleration**:

**First equation**: v = u + at
- Relates velocity, acceleration, and time
- v = final velocity, u = initial velocity, a = acceleration, t = time

**Second equation**: s = ut + ½at²
- Relates displacement, initial velocity, acceleration, and time
- s = displacement

**Third equation**: v² = u² + 2as
- Relates velocity, acceleration, and displacement
- Derived by eliminating time from first two equations

## 7.6 Derivation of Equations of Motion

- From definition of acceleration: a = (v - u)/t → v = u + ut
- From average velocity: s = ½(u + v)t → substituting v = u + at → s = ut + ½at²
- From first two equations: substituting t = (v - u)/a → v² = u² + 2as

## 7.7 Graphical Representation of Motion

### Distance-Time Graph
- **Uniform speed**: Straight line passing through origin
  - Slope of line = speed
  - Steeper slope = higher speed
- **Non-uniform speed**: Curved line
  - Slope at any point = instantaneous speed
- **At rest**: Straight line parallel to time axis
  - Slope = 0 (speed = 0)

### Velocity-Time Graph
- **Uniform acceleration**: Straight line with non-zero slope
  - Slope of line = acceleration
  - Steeper slope = higher acceleration
- **Non-uniform acceleration**: Curved line
  - Slope at any point = instantaneous acceleration
- **Zero acceleration (uniform velocity)**: Straight line parallel to time axis
  - Slope = 0 (acceleration = 0)
- **Distance covered** = Area under velocity-time graph
  - For uniform acceleration: area of trapezium = ½(u + v)t

## 7.8 Uniform Circular Motion

- **Uniform circular motion**: Motion in a circular path with constant speed
  - Speed is constant but velocity changes continuously (direction changes)
  - Acceleration is always directed towards the center of the circle (centripetal acceleration)
  - Direction of motion is always tangent to the circle

- **Time period (T)**: Time taken for one complete revolution
  - T = 2πr / v
  - r = radius of circle, v = speed

- **Frequency (f)**: Number of revolutions per second
  - f = 1/T
  - SI unit: Hertz (Hz)

- **Speed in circular motion**: v = 2πr / T = 2πrf
  - Example: An object moving in a circle of radius 7 m with speed 22 m/s
  - Time period = 2πr/v = 2 × 22/7 × 7 / 22 = 2 s
  - Frequency = 1/2 = 0.5 Hz

## Activities

**Activity 7.1**: Move along a straight line and observe motion - motion is relative to reference point
**Activity 7.2**: Measure distance and displacement of a ball thrown up and caught - distance > displacement if ball returns
**Activity 7.3**: Roll a ball on a tilted surface - observe non-uniform acceleration (speed increases as it rolls down)
**Activity 7.4**: Plot distance-time graph for a toy car - straight line = uniform speed, curved line = non-uniform speed
**Activity 7.5**: Move a stone in a circle while tied to a thread - observe that direction keeps changing even though speed is constant
